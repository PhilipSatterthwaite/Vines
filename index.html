Vines

How to play

A word is hidden in the ring. It uses every letter on the ring, and it can use a letter more than once, so you don't know how long it is.

  1. Guess a 5-letter word using ring letters (repeats allowed). For each pair of neighbouring letters in your guess, a vine grows:

    If the hidden word is BALLOON: B→A and A→L sit side by side in that order, so they grow. A→N and N→A don't, so they wither. Direction matters, and a doubled letter like LL grows a loop.

  2. Guess the word whenever you think you have it: any word longer than 5 letters that uses every ring letter. It only tells you right or wrong.
  3. You have 6 guesses in total, shared by both kinds. Your last one has to be a guess at the word.

A new puzzle every day.
