Vines

How to play

You get a crossword clue and five blanks. The answer is a 5-letter word, and you have six guesses.

  1. Guess any 5-letter word. Wherever two neighbouring letters in your guess also sit side by side in the answer, in that order, they turn green and a vine grows up to fill them in.

    If the answer is PLANT, SLANT grows L→A, A→N and N→T, filling in L, A, N and T. S→L withers, because SL isn't in PLANT.

  2. Yellow letters are in the answer but didn't grow a vine. They're just hints, and fill nothing in.

    TOPAZ grows nothing in PLANT, but T, P and A are all in it somewhere.

  3. Fill every blank to win. Know the answer? Just guess it: every pair matches, so it fills in all at once.

A new puzzle every day. Guesses can be any word Wordle accepts. Clues come from past New York Times crosswords.
