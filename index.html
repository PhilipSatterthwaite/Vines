Vines

Long-press or right-click a letter to cross it out

How to play

A word of 6 to 9 letters is hidden in the ring. Every one of its letters is different, and all of them are on the ring. Its length is shown above the ring.

  1. Guess a 5-letter word using ring letters (repeats allowed). For each pair of neighbouring letters in your guess, a vine grows:

    If the hidden word is PLANTER: L→A, T→E and E→R sit side by side in that order, so they grow. A→T doesn't, so it withers. Direction matters: T→A would wither too.

  2. Guess the whole word whenever you think you have it. It only tells you right or wrong.
  3. You have 6 guesses in total, and both kinds use one. Every word you spend on vines is one less chance to answer.
SproutThe ring is exactly the word's letters.
VineOne extra letter is a decoy.
ThicketTwo extra letters are decoys.

A new puzzle in each mode every day. Long-press or right-click a ring letter to cross it out.
